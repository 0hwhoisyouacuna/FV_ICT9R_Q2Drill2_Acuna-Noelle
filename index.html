OBMC Home Page


A school made to educate children from pre-k to 12, combining the tradition and innovation to prepare a child for real life. Nurturing in the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori, our goal is to turn our students into individuals who can succeed and influence the future of our changing world.


We offer quality Montessori education to the thriving families, with our school branches offering students to work productively and explore our vibrant community.
